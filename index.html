📘 نکتهٔ کوتاه دستور زبان انگلیسی

Present Simple: برای عادت‌ها و واقعیت‌های عمومی استفاده می‌شود.

ساختار: Subject + base verb (برای he/she/it معمولاً فعل s می‌گیرد).

مثال: I study every day. — من هر روز درس می‌خوانم.

پښتو: زه هره ورځ زده کړه کوم.

تلفظ با قابلیت گفتار مرورگر انجام می‌شود و کیفیت صدا به دستگاه و زبان‌های نصب‌شده بستگی دارد. این بخش فعلاً آموزش مقدماتی است و ترجمهٔ ماشینی یا معلم زندهٔ هوش مصنوعی نیست.

Afghanistan National Reconstruction & Sustainable Development Program (2026–2031)

Purpose. A proposed multi-sector framework to identify, prepare and support viable projects that improve essential services, strengthen livelihoods, expand productive capacity and promote resilient infrastructure in Afghanistan.

Proposed investment themes

  • Water security, irrigation rehabilitation and climate-resilient agriculture.
  • Distributed renewable energy for essential public services and local enterprises.
  • Basic health-service infrastructure, essential equipment and supply-chain capacity.
  • Technical and vocational education linked to labour-market demand.
  • Agro-processing, storage, cold-chain logistics and market access.
  • Safe rural connectivity and transport links to essential services and markets.

Project preparation and investment discipline

Each project requires a defined location and beneficiary profile, technical and economic feasibility, land and legal due diligence, environmental and social assessment, transparent procurement, lifecycle operating costs, a financing structure, measurable indicators and a reviewable risk register.

Potential financing approaches

Subject to project suitability and applicable law, options may include public resources, private investment, transparent public–private arrangements, grants or development finance. No institution, investor or public authority is represented as a partner or committed funder without written authorization and documented agreement.

Governance and safeguards

The proposed approach emphasizes competitive procurement, conflict-of-interest disclosure, independent technical and financial review, environmental and social safeguards, accessible community feedback, public progress reporting and clear long-term operation and maintenance responsibilities.

Indicative implementation sequence

  1. 2026 – Preparation: needs assessment, consultation, project screening and feasibility work.
  2. 2027–2028 – Initial implementation: phased delivery of projects that meet readiness and safeguard requirements.
  3. 2029–2030 – Evidence-led expansion: scale approaches that demonstrate results, affordability and sustainability.
  4. 2031 – Evaluation: independent assessment, public reporting and planning for continued maintenance.

Next steps for prospective technical and financing partners

Expressions of interest may identify relevant expertise, eligibility requirements, due-diligence standards, preferred financing instruments and documentation required for formal project review. Any engagement remains subject to independent verification, applicable law and written agreement.

Important notice: Project locations, costs, beneficiaries, schedules, expected returns and financing gaps have not yet been independently validated. No funding commitment, approval, endorsement or partnership is implied.
